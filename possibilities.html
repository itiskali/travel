Around Sat 30 Jan (arrival day)

Tokyo

  • Shimokitazawa Tengu Festival

    Likely weekend of 30 to 31 Jan 2027 · Shimokitazawa shopping streets, around Shinryuji Temple

    In 2026 it ran 30 Jan to 1 Feb, main parade on Saturday afternoon. 2027 dates not confirmed. A local Setsubun festival with a parade carrying a giant red tengu head and bean throwing. Area has vintage shops and cafes.

  • Early plum blossoms at Kyōdo-no-Mori

    Late Jan to early Mar 2027

    Blooms start late Jan, best around mid-Feb. Entry ¥300. Quieter than the big plum spots.

  • Setsubun at Tokyo Solamachi

    Late Jan to early Feb 2027 · Tokyo Solamachi, Skytree Town

    Bean-throwing event, free.

  • Plum festivals at Yushima Tenjin and Kameido Tenjin

    Usually open in early Feb · Yushima / Kameido

    Probably starts after 30 Jan. Could fit the end of the trip instead.

Near Tokyo

Day or overnight trips from Tokyo

  • Hakone, one night

    Any time · Hakone, southwest of Tokyo

    Winter air means clearer Fuji views. Ryokan with outdoor bath, ropeway. Could be a soft landing after the flight or a last night before Haneda. Very touristy.

Nagano

Near the snow monkeys

  • Matsumoto

    Any time in winter · Matsumoto, Nagano Prefecture

    50 min from Nagano by Limited Express Shinano. Original castle with Alps behind, Nakamachi Street (old storehouses turned into cafes and craft shops), base for winter hikes and mountain onsen. Fits wellness and crafts.

Hokkaido — only works if Hokkaido is the last leg, flying back to Haneda on 12 Feb

Hokkaido

  • Otaru Snow Light Path

    6 to 13 Feb 2027 (confirmed), evenings · Otaru canal and the former Temiya railway line

    About 40 min by train from Sapporo. Quieter, more intimate than Sapporo's festival. Free.

  • Noboribetsu Onsen, overnight

    Any time in winter · Southwest Hokkaido

    About 1h10 from Sapporo by limited express plus a local bus, or a direct bus (1h40 to 2h). Nine kinds of spring water, Jigokudani (Hell Valley) walk. Some paths can close in winter.

  • Sapporo Snow Festival

    4 to 11 Feb 2027 (confirmed) · Odori Park, Susukino, Tsudome

    Big crowds, more of a tourist spectacle. Overlaps with Otaru 6 to 11 Feb.

Yamagata — a separate trip north of Tokyo

Yamagata

  • Zao snow monsters (juhyo)

    Season usually late Dec to late Feb. 2027 light-up dates not yet confirmed · Zao Onsen, Yamagata

    Frost-covered trees seen by ropeway, snowshoe, or night light-up. Zao Onsen is also a hot spring town. Genuinely unique in winter.

  • Ginzan Onsen

    Winter · Northern Yamagata

    Wooden inns in deep snow. Very popular. Recent winters limited day trippers (shuttle bus, evening tickets); staying overnight avoids this. Check whether rules repeat for 2027.

Food note from the Feb article: the article's food picks (crab, Hida beef, Yonezawa beef, beef tongue, ramen) don't work for celiac or vegetarian. Ignore them.
