Japan in winter

Kalindi and Lisa · 29 January – 13 February 2027

Your photos show on this device only. Send them to Claude to add them for good.

Two weeks across three legs: an onsen village under a ski mountain, teahouses and crafts in Kanazawa, powder snow and candlelit canals in Hokkaido, bookended by Ginza. Fifteen days, five places.

Where we're going

  1. Sat 30 Jan 1 night

    Tokyo · arrival

    Soft landing in Ginza. Coffee, first safe meal of the trip, early night.

    Day 1 →

  2. Sun 31 Jan – Tue 2 Feb 3 nights

    Nozawa Onsen · Nagano

    A wooden hot-spring village under a ski mountain. Free public baths, snow monkeys at Jigokudani, snowshoeing through old beech forest.

    Day 2 →

  3. Wed 3 – Thu 4 Feb 2 nights

    Kanazawa

    Teahouse districts in snow. Setsubun bean-throwing at Utasu Shrine, Kenroku-en in its winter rope cones, gold leaf and tea.

    Day 5 →

  4. Fri 5 – Sun 7 Feb 3 nights

    Niseko · Hokkaido

    Powder snow under Mt Yotei. Guided snowshoe to frozen Hangetsu Lake, long onsen soaks, dinner with a friend.

    Day 7 →

  5. Mon 8 – Tue 9 Feb 2 nights

    Otaru

    A port town lit by thousands of snow lanterns during the Snow Light Path festival. Glassblowing on the canal.

    Day 10 →

  6. Wed 10 – Fri 12 Feb 3 nights

    Tokyo · return

    Back to Ginza for shopping, one last craft workshop, and a plum day at Atami or in the city.
