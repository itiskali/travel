30 Jan, 10 to 12 Feb

Tokyo

Kintsugi

  • Kuge Crafts Studio

    Near Shin-Koenji.

    About 2.5 hours, family-run, simple English with an English-speaking son, repair antique chipped pottery. Tea and sweets included. Veltra

  • mon cadeau

    Shibuya.

    Small English-language classes, modern kintsugi, one piece finished and taken home the same day. Klook

  • Roppongi or Jiyugaoka

    Bring your own dish, 2 hours, take it home the same day, skin-friendly materials. Veltra 1 · Veltra 2

  • MUSUBI

    Repair an antique Japanese ceramic, small group, professional studio. Klook

Note: traditional urushi lacquer takes weeks to cure. Same-day classes generally use faster modern materials. Ask which one you're getting.

Edo kiriko (glass cutting, a Tokyo craft)

  • Sokichi (Asakusa)

    About 90 min (120 for the long course), Japanese, English and Chinese. Veltra

  • Sumida Edo Kiriko-kan

    Cut traditional patterns into a glass you choose, English available. Go Tokyo

Woodblock printing

  • Mokuhankan (Asakusa)

    "Print party" where you print your own woodblock print, near Senso-ji. Japan Travel

  • Takahashi Kobo (Bunkyo)

    Japan's oldest ukiyo-e studio, 160+ years, print onto fans you take home. Private and high-end. Matcha

Washi papermaking

  • Ozu Washi (Nihonbashi)

    Tokyo's washi house since 1653, about 45 min, A4 sheet, take it home the same day, big paper shop after. Go Tokyo

  • Asakusa, Tosa washi

    About 1 hour in English, you wear samue work clothes. Trip to Japan

Sashiko

  • canoha (Ogikubo)

    Run by Keiko, teaching since 2017, 2 hours in English. Make a stitched cloth (fukin), or try visible mending. You leave with cloth, thread and needles to keep going at home. KKday 1 · KKday 2

31 Jan to 3 Feb

Nozawa and Iiyama

Uchiyama washi, Iiyama (fits Wed 3 Feb, on the way to Kanazawa)

A local paper made for 350+ years and bleached on snow. Designated traditional craft.

  • Iiyama Handicraft Paper Studio

    About 10 min walk from Iiyama Station. Make postcards, bookmarks or coloured paper. Simple English. 10 to 20 min plus drying. Irregular closing days, so book ahead through the Iiyama tourism bureau. Iiyama tourism · Go Nagano

  • Nozawa handmade washi workshop

    Nozawa itself also lists one. Nozawa Hospitality

Akebi vine basket weaving (Nozawa's own craft)

Vines harvested in the mountains and softened in Ogama, the village's hottest spring. About 2 hours to make a basket.

  • Book through your inn

    Easiest to book through wherever you stay in Nozawa, or ask at the village tourist information centre on arrival.

  • Nozawa Hospitality (English)

    Traditional crafts workshop, 2 to 10 people, all year. Booked through their concierge, for guests of their 15 properties only. Details

  • Matsuya ryokan

    Vine craft for guests, make a basket in about 2 hours. Matsuya

Kintsugi

  • Rin Kiyose

    Fourth generation of a Kaga maki-e lacquer family, private session for 1 to 6 people in a restored storehouse at IN KANAZAWA HOUSE, about 75 min, English. Japan Travel

  • kintsugian

    Traditional urushi lacquer, small group, English. Not recommended for sensitive skin. Airbnb

5 to 7 Feb

Niseko

None of the five researched crafts were found here. Local options instead (kept here only, not in the itinerary):

  • Wool felting and spinning (Kutchan)

    In a pottery studio in a former school, at the foot of Mt Yotei. Sessions at 10:00 and 14:00, about 2 hours. KKday

  • Hand-built pottery

    Same studio. Pieces need firing, so they're shipped or collected later. KKday

  • Takumi Woodwork (Kutchan)

    Short making sessions with traditional tools. Open Monday to Friday only, no booking contact listed. Niseko Tourism
