Atami Plum Garden (Atami Baien)

  • Strongest option for both dates. Some of the earliest blooming plum varieties in Japan.

  • Nearly 500 trees in about 59 varieties. Early, mid and late bloomers flower in turn, so something is out across the whole festival.

  • 2027 festival confirmed: 9 Jan to 7 Mar 2027. Hours 8:30 to 16:00. Entry ¥300 at the door.

  • Wellness

    Atami is a hot spring town. The garden has footbaths during the festival.

  • Getting there

    Tokyo Station to Atami by shinkansen, about 40 to 50 minutes (check the timetable). Then a 10-minute walk from JR Kinomiya Station (Ito Line), or a 10-minute taxi from JR Atami Station.

  • Address

    8-11 Baiencho, Atami, Shizuoka.

Yugawara Plum Grove (Ume no Utage)

  • About 4,000 trees on a hillside above Yugawara.

  • 2027 festival scheduled: 6 Feb to 7 Mar 2027. Entry about ¥300.

  • Yugawara is one stop from Atami on the JR line, so Atami and Yugawara can be one day around 12 Feb.

Tokyo city spots

Most Tokyo plum festivals open in early February, but the best viewing is usually mid to late February. 12 Feb is borderline. 30 Jan is early.

  • Shiba Park

    Earliest in the city, blooms late Jan to early Mar. Plum blossoms with Tokyo Tower behind them. Free.

  • Yushima Tenjin

    The most atmosphere. Popular since the Edo period, about 300 trees. The festival has shamisen playing, plum wine tasting, traditional dance, tea and stalls. Open 8:00 to 19:30, free. 3-minute walk from Yushima Station (Chiyoda Line), near Ueno. 2027 dates not yet confirmed.

  • Hanegi Park (Setagaya Plum Festival)

    The largest in the city, about 600 trees. Lively on weekends with music and stalls. 15 minutes from Shinjuku on the Odakyu Line.

  • Kyōdo-no-Mori

    Open-air museum, a quieter, more relaxed setting. Flowers start late Jan, best around mid-Feb. ¥300.

  • Kameido Tenjin

    Shrine plum festival, early Feb to early Mar 2027.
